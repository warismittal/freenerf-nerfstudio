\section{Experiments}
\textbf{Data and protocol.} NeRF-synthetic Lego and Chair \cite{mildenhall2020nerf}, downscaled to $400\times400$. The 8 training views are DietNeRF's \cite{jain2021dietnerf} (indices 26, 86, 2, 55, 75, 93, 16, 73, re-derived from its seeded sampler); the test set is every 8th test image (25 views). The 100-view reference uses all training images. The self-captured scene is a laptop on a desk, photographed with a phone (76 photos, both orientations, centre-cropped to square). COLMAP (pycolmap, one shared camera) registered \RealRegistered{} of them with a mean reprojection error of \RealReproj\,px; 14 registered views are held out for testing and the 8 training views are chosen by farthest-point sampling from the remaining \RealPool.
\textbf{Training.} Every arm uses the same budget: \Iters{} iterations of 4096 rays with \AmpText{} on one Kaggle T4 (\SPerIter\,s/iteration measured by the job itself before any reported run). Seeds are fixed and written to every log; the main comparison is repeated over \MainSeeds{} seeds, ablations use one seed.
\textbf{Arms.} A: vanilla NeRF; D: FreeNeRF (mask + occlusion); B: mask only; C: occlusion only; E: RegNeRF depth smoothness; A$'$: vanilla with nerfstudio's untuned defaults.
