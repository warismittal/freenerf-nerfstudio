\section{Changes from the proposal}\label{sec:changes}
\textbf{Gates.} Gate 0 (literature audit) passed in the proposal. Gate 1 (harness): the method, the evaluator and the controls C1--C9 are implemented with 74 unit and integration tests, the 8-view split was verified against DietNeRF's sampler, and $\tau$ was measured on the actual GPU (\SPerIter\,s/iteration) to fix $I=\Iters$ by the budget rule. \GateTwoText
\textbf{What was dropped, and why.} The proposal planned 82 standard-run equivalents ($\approx$123 GPU-hours) on an assumed GPU; the free Kaggle quota is 30 GPU-hours a week on two T4s, and several sessions were lost to Kaggle's idle shutdown. We therefore (i) dropped the 36 Optuna tuning runs and used the paper's hyper-parameters for every arm (equal, zero tuning budget, which keeps the comparison fair); (ii) reduced the matrix to \NumCells{} cells: 3 seeds for the main A/D comparison, one seed for the ablation, the comparator and the view sweep $N\in\{4,8,16\}$ on Lego only; (iii) dropped the stretch controls X1/X2 (reversed and step masks) and the 32-view point; (iv) ran the reproduction check (E1) on Drums only. Runs that did not fit are listed in \texttt{results/PROGRESS.md}.
\textbf{What was added.} Mixed-precision training (to fit the budget; the job verifies the trained weights are finite), random-background training for Chair (Section~5), a resume path that re-uses finished checkpoints, and per-run preview images.
\textbf{Outcome against the pre-declared direction.} The proposal fixed one primary hypothesis before any reported scene was trained, H2: FreeNeRF recovers $\rho_\mathrm{PSNR}\ge0.30$ of the gap with an interval above zero and lowers LPIPS by $\ge0.05$ on both Lego and Chair. Its status is \textbf{\HTwoStatus}. The reproduction verdict on the proposal's scale is \emph{\ReproVerdict}. We report this as measured, including where it falls short of the published 13.93$\to$24.26\,dB result; the main differences are the base model (nerfstudio vanilla NeRF vs.\ mip-NeRF), the much shorter training and the protocol details we could not match.
